## CS50AI

Repository for completed projects submitted as part of CS50's Introduction to Artificial Intelligence with Python.

Reflecting back on this entire course, it was an excellent companion to my studies at University, and taught me a lot more about Python, building upon what I had learned previously in CS50x. It was a good mix of classic programming, and getting some hands-on experience with ML libraries that I hadn't used before.

At many times challenging, but rewarding a great sense of accomplishment and respect for those who have pioneered in the field for the last few decades. So many hidden layers of algorithms, breakthroughs, and technologies that have lead to where we are now in the industry (no pun intended!)
